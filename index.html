Calculation Formulas
Steel Scrap kg (base material — fills furnace heat weight balance)
Steel Scrap = TapWeight − ReturnsKg − AllFurnaceAlloys

Reduced Si aim (E35) — accounts for ladle Si contributions
E35 = Si_aim − (LadleSi_contributed_to_Total_Weight)

Alloy Calculation (Accounting for Returns in CHARGE mode)
Required_Alloy = (Heat×Target% − Heat×Base% − ReturnsKg×(Return% − Base%)) / AlloyPurity%

Carbon Equivalent (CE)
CE = C% + (Si% ÷ 3) + (P% ÷ 3)

How to Use
CHARGE Mode — Planning the furnace melt
  1. Select CHARGE MODE
  2. Select your Target Grade (*) and enter Target Heat Weight (*).
  3. In Step 2, define your Returns. You can pick a saved grade or manually enter a custom chemistry. Enter the weight you plan to charge.
  4. The calculator automatically predicts the full furnace additions needed, balances the rest with Steel Scrap, and also provides your planned Ladle Additions upfront.
  5. You can manually override any recommended addition by editing the dashed input boxes in Step 3.
TRIM Mode — Ladle treatment after furnace tap
  1. Select TRIM MODE
  2. Enter the actual Bath Weight. By default it uses your target weight, but update this if your tap was heavier/lighter.
  3. Enter actual spectrometer results (C, Si, Mn, P, Mo, Cu, Mg, Ba). The calculator correctly assumes this reading *already includes* your physical Returns and Steel Scrap.
  4. Calculator evaluates chemistry gaps purely on Actual vs Target and provides the remaining Furnace & Ladle additions needed to correct the batch.
