Steel Ladle Practice

Aluminium and FeTi go in at tap, not in the furnace. CHARGE mode still solves the amount from the grade's Al/Ti spec (using each alloy's own recovery — Al 88%, FeTi 85%) but shows them as LADLE additions. In TRIM the furnace correction ignores Al/Ti entirely — they were planned at charge and are delivered after the bath is trimmed — and the required ladle top-up is shown as a note. Elements the grade only caps (ending in *) are never dosed.

Iron Ladle Practice

These values now drive the solver's ladle additions directly for iron grades (Mg-bearing SG irons like I10 / I10H).

FeSiMg dose blank = auto-calculated from the grade's Mg aim and the Mg recovery. When you enter an explicit dose, the Mg spec check becomes advisory (shown in the prediction table) instead of a hard solver constraint, since you've chosen the treatment dose yourself. The Grey/T-Series inoculant is shown as a practice note on T-grades (not added to the optimization).
